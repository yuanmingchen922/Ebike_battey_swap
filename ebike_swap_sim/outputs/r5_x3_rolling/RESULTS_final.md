# X3 (M7) rolling horizon: foresight vs coordination, final report

Source: `outputs/r5_x3_rolling/records.jsonl` (210 records, last record per resume key; no key has more than one
record). Tables and figures are regenerated by `figs/make_x3_final.py` (reads the records only, no solves) and the
machine-readable numbers are in `figs/x3_final_tables.json`. Protocol: aggregated E2 ILP, Threads 3, Seed 0,
TimeLimit 300 s offline / 60 s per rolling window (W 24 slots, commit k 6 slots, 8 windows), reachable pruning, demand
hash identical across methods for every instance. Costs are USD per 4 h window on the M1 end-adjusted basis
(`total_end_adjusted` = `total` + `C_end_adjust`); the headline uses relaxed end targets (rho = 0, R_end = 0);
written targets give the price of recovery. Intervals are exact Student-t 95 % CIs over seeds (n - 1 dof), shown as
[lower, upper]; every difference is paired by seed. `best_rule` = per-instance minimum over r2, r5_range and r5
(r5_transfer does not apply because these instances have no services).

Decomposition, per instance: `best_rule - offline = (rolling - offline) + (best_rule - rolling)`, i.e. headline gap =
value of information (foresight) + value of coordination online. Share retained online = `(best_rule - rolling) /
(best_rule - offline)`.

## Summary

Most of the offline coordination gap is still there when the coordinator does not know the future. With a 10 min
lookahead, the rolling-horizon ILP costs 648.64 USD on usd4h_ebaas (n = 10) and 944.07 USD on r5_stress4h (n = 5).
The best rule costs 830.91 and 1387.36 USD and the full-foresight offline ILP costs 582.05 and 826.10 USD. The rolling
ILP beats the best rule on all 15 instances. The online value of coordination is 182.27 USD [80.50, 284.04] (19.94 %
of the best-rule cost) and 443.29 USD [250.53, 636.04] (31.26 %). Of the offline gap (28.28 % [20.91, 35.64] and
39.96 % [33.58, 46.35]), rolling keeps 0.64 [0.44, 0.83] and 0.77 [0.64, 0.90] at lambda 10 (mean of per-seed ratios;
ratio of means 0.73 and 0.79). Foresight (rolling minus offline) is the smaller part at lambda 10: 66.59 USD [43.25, 89.92] (11.43 %
of offline; 8.33 % of the best rule) and 117.97 USD [73.13, 162.82] (14.35 %; 8.70 %), i.e. 27 % and 21 % of the gap
as a ratio of means (mean of per-seed shares 0.36 [0.17, 0.56] and 0.23 [0.10, 0.36]). This is clear on r5_stress4h
(5/5 seeds) but only borderline on usd4h_ebaas, where foresight exceeds online coordination on 3 of 10 seeds and the
share interval reaches 0.56. With no lookahead (lambda 0) foresight rises to 107.90 and 201.85 USD (13.34 % and 14.66 %
of the best rule) and the retained share falls to 0.43 and 0.63; on usd4h_ebaas foresight and online coordination are
then about equal (foresight share 0.57 [0.29, 0.85] mean of ratios, 0.43 ratio of means; 14.93 % vs 13.34 % of the
best rule). The mean online cost falls from lambda 0 to 10 on both families (9/10 and 5/5 seeds). On usd4h_ebaas it
falls again from 10 to 20 min (lambda 20 retains 0.81), but that step is small and not robust (28.54 USD [2.17, 54.90],
7/10 seeds, paired t p = 0.04, Wilcoxon p = 0.06). On r5_stress4h no further gain beyond 10 min is detected
(-4.36 USD [-59.58, 50.85], 3/5 seeds still improve), which with n = 5 neither shows nor rules out a plateau. The forecast-based battery plan adds no measurable value (6.75 USD
[-8.32, 21.83] and 9.71 USD [-8.95, 28.36]).

Two caveats apply to the online numbers. First, 47 of the 60 online runs used the terminal fallback, which leaves
the terminal certificate (9) unpriced. Second, part of the online gain comes from turning down orders: online
policies serve fewer orders than the rules but avoid almost all lateness. The price of recovery (written - relaxed)
is small offline and is not measured online. On usd4h_ebaas (seeds 0-4, all solves OPTIMAL) it is a resolved,
negligible quantity: -0.03 USD [-0.18, 0.12] end-adjusted and +0.29 USD [0.17, 0.40] on the unadjusted objective
(positive on every seed, under 0.1 % of cost). On r5_stress4h (6.73 USD [-3.87, 17.33]) it is within the MIP gaps of
the time-limited written solves. Online the terminal fallback does not enforce the written targets.

## Anomalies

1. **Terminal fallback in the online runs.** In 47 of the 60 relaxed online runs (28/40 on usd4h_ebaas, 19/20 on
   r5_stress4h), every window that reaches H (t0 = 24, 30, 36, 42) was infeasible and was solved again with a
   penalised dummy terminal arc. The stitched schedules then break the terminal certificate (9) for 0.8-2.6 bikes on
   average. `C_dummy_terminal` is 0 in every record, so the evaluated cost does not price this, while the offline ILP
   enforces (9). The rules also break (9) without a penalty in some runs (r2: 4/5 r5_stress4h seeds, 1/10
   usd4h_ebaas). The online costs are therefore somewhat too favourable: the value of information is understated and
   the online coordination value is overstated, by an amount this run cannot measure.
2. **The rolling price of recovery is not measured.** Because of the same fallback, the written targets are not
   enforced in the windows that reach H. Rolling with written targets is identical to rolling with relaxed targets on
   8 of 9 seeds. The one seed without the fallback (usd4h_ebaas seed 4) costs 13.72 USD more with written targets.
3. **The offline price of recovery is within the MIP gap on r5_stress4h.** Four of the five written-target offline
   solves hit TIME_LIMIT, and their MIP start was not accepted. The per-seed recovery of 3.19 / 2.94 / 6.01 / 21.51 USD
   is about the size of the gaps (seed 4: 2.52 % gap, about 21 USD).
4. **The written usd4h headline reflects the seed subset.** 21.67 % on seeds 0-4 against 28.28 % on seeds 0-9; the
   relaxed value on seeds 0-4 is also 21.67 %.
5. **Lookahead is not monotone per seed.** lambda 0 >= 10 >= 20 fails on 4/10 usd4h_ebaas and 2/5 r5_stress4h seeds.
   lambda 0 -> 10 improves on 9/10 and 5/5 seeds (usd4h_ebaas 41.31 [22.62, 60.00], t p = 0.0007; r5_stress4h 83.87
   [48.77, 118.98], t p = 0.003). lambda 10 -> 20 improves on only 7/10 and 3/5 seeds (usd4h_ebaas 28.54 [2.17, 54.90],
   t p = 0.04, Wilcoxon p = 0.06; r5_stress4h -4.36 [-59.58, 50.85], t p = 0.84). On r5_stress4h seed 0, lambda 20
   (963.09) is worse than lambda 0 (942.88). Per-seed non-monotonicity is consistent with the myopic-window note in
   DECISIONS (round-5 usd4h probe), but this run does not test that mechanism.
6. **Online policies serve slightly fewer orders than the rules on average.** Served share is 0.856 vs 0.919
   (usd4h_ebaas; paired difference 0.064 [-0.002, 0.130], p = 0.057, rolling lower on 7/10 seeds) and 0.729 vs 0.754
   (r5_stress4h; 0.025 [-0.062, 0.112], p = 0.47, rolling lower on only 2/5 seeds), so the served-share difference is
   not significant per seed. The lateness difference is: rolling avoids almost all lateness (5.7 vs 125.7 USD, paired
   120.0 [53.2, 186.8], 10/10 seeds; 5.4 vs 273.6 USD, 268.2 [163.8, 372.6], 5/5 seeds). The rules have no reject
   option.
7. **Rolling barely charges batteries.** Electricity cost is about 0.02 USD against 0.71 USD for the rules, with 5.7
   vs 9.2 swaps. The end-of-window energy deficit is priced only through `C_end_adjust`, which is 1-2 USD for every
   method (0.2 % of cost) and does not change any ranking.
8. **On usd4h_ebaas seed 0 the online gain is almost zero.** Rolling lambda 10 retains 0.05 of a small 12.87 % gap,
   and rolling lambda 0 (650.51) is worse than the best rule (613.10). This is the only instance where an online
   policy loses to the rule.
9. **The r5 rule equals r5_range on all 15 instances** (no services), so best_rule = min(r2, r5_range): r5_range on
   4/10 and 4/5 seeds, r2 on the rest.
10. **The forecast plan used one bootstrap scenario per epoch** (budget setting), and its value is not significant.
    On usd4h_ebaas seeds 0-2 the forecast run reproduces plain rolling to within 0.3 USD.
11. **One relaxed offline solve is TIME_LIMIT** (r5_stress4h seed 2, gap 0.09 %, about 0.8 USD). Its value of
    information is a difference of upper bounds; the effect is negligible.
12. **Comparison with rounds 3-4.** usd4h_ebaas uses the eBaaS energy calibration (L 24). The round-3/4 usd4h
    headline of 29.55 % +/- 5.61 (n = 30) is not like-for-like with it, but it agrees (28.28 % [20.91, 35.64]).

## Interpretation

The offline E2 headline is not mainly a value of perfect foresight. Across both families, between about two thirds
and four fifths of the gap between the best rule and the offline ILP is still there when the ILP sees orders only 10
min before release and re-plans every 30 min. Foresight is worth 8-9 % of the best-rule cost at lambda 10 (about 21-36 % of the gap; clear on r5_stress4h, only
borderline on usd4h_ebaas, where it exceeds online coordination on 3 of 10 seeds). Online coordination is worth 20 % on
usd4h_ebaas and 31 % on the energy-stressed r5_stress4h family, so coordination matters more when batteries bind.
Foresight matters most at lambda 0 (13-15 % of the best-rule cost), where on usd4h_ebaas it is as large as online
coordination. The step from 0 to 10 min of lookahead is clearly beneficial on both families; further steps are not
established (usd4h_ebaas 10 -> 20 min marginal, 7/10 seeds; r5_stress4h no detectable gain with n = 5), and the
per-seed curves are not monotone. The battery-side forecast plan adds nothing
measurable: once dispatch is coordinated, the station layer is not where the remaining online loss sits on these
instances. The online advantage over the rules comes mostly from avoiding lateness through order admission, together
with shorter responses. Rolling turns down more orders than the rules and still pays less, so the result depends on
how the model prices unserved orders against late ones; the manuscript should say this. The online costs are also
somewhat too favourable, because most online runs fall back to an unpriced dummy terminal arc in the last windows.
The retained shares are therefore upper bounds, and closing the terminal fallback (pricing (9) or a terminal reserve)
is the main open fix. The price of recovery is negligible and resolved offline on usd4h_ebaas (-0.03 USD end-adjusted, +0.29 USD on the
objective, all OPTIMAL), unresolved on r5_stress4h (within the MIP gaps), and not measured online because the
fallback does not enforce the written targets. With n = 5 on r5_stress4h, the intervals are wide (for
example coordination 250-636 USD), but every instance points the same way.
