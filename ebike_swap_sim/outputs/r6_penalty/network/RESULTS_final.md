# R6 network: value of the swap network and of coordination under the new prices (final report)

Question: under the new prices (P 60 USD per unserved order, w 18 USD/h from release, v 15 USD/h from the promise),
how much do the swap network (vs home charging) and coordination (best rule vs relaxed aggregated ILP) each save?
The design is the round-5 2 x 2 (home charging vs swap network) x (best rule vs ILP) on usd4h x1 (n = 10, seeds 0-9)
and stress4h (n = 5, seeds 0-4). The report covers the total effect, the Shapley split, the network value under each
policy (including whether it is negative on any seed) and productivity, and compares each with round 5 (old prices
P 30, v 36).

Sources: `outputs/r6_penalty/network/records.jsonl` (165 records) and, for the old prices,
`outputs/r5_x4_network/records.jsonl` (180 records). For each key (case, seed, infra, method) the last record is used.
Neither file has duplicate keys. Every number below was recomputed from the records by `figs/make_network_final.py`.
The tables were generated by `figs/make_network_tables.py`, and no solves were run. I checked the auto-generated
`RESULTS.md` against this recomputation: its numbers reproduce to the printed precision, but its anomaly list is
incomplete (see P2).

Conventions:
- All tables use relaxed end targets and the end-adjusted cost (`total + C_end_adjust`) unless stated otherwise.
- Best rule = the per-instance minimum over r2, M2 and M2+M3 of the end-adjusted total. M3 alone is not applicable
  because these instances have no transport services.
- Network value = home - network, under the same policy. Coordination value = best rule - ILP, on the same
  infrastructure. On the network the coordination value is the paper's gap, 100 (best rule - ILP) / best rule, computed
  per instance and then averaged.
- Interaction = coordination value on the network - coordination value at home.
- Shapley network = (network value under best rule + network value under ILP) / 2. Shapley coordination is defined in
  the same way. The two Shapley shares add up to the total effect = (home, best rule) - (network, ILP).
- Intervals are exact Student-t 95 % CIs over seeds, written `mean [lo, hi]`. Differences are always paired per seed.
  The critical t is 2.262 for n = 10 and 2.776 for n = 5.
- "Seeds k/n" counts the seeds on which the quantity is positive.

## 1. Problems and anomalies

- **P1. Three of the five stress4h network ILPs stopped at the time limit.** This is new in round 6.
  - The relaxed network ILP on stress4h reached the 300 s limit on seeds 1, 2 and 3, with MIP gaps of 1.12 %, 0.31 %
    and 0.43 % (incumbent vs bound). In round 5, all 5 were OPTIMAL at the same limit, the slowest at 294 s.
  - The new prices make the network ILP harder. Seeds 0 and 4 needed 105 s and 192 s, against 20-98 s on usd4h.
  - Effect on this report: on stress4h the network ILP cost is an upper bound. The network value under the ILP, the
    coordination value on the network and the total effect are therefore slightly understated.
  - Size of that effect: replacing each incumbent by its MIP bound raises the network value under the ILP from
    751.1 to 754.8 USD on average. The largest single change is +11.6 USD (seed 1). The coordination value on the
    network rises from 639.8 to 643.5 USD. No conclusion below changes.
  - The psweep stage solved the same 5 instances at P 60 and found identical incumbents on all 5 seeds.
- **P2. The auto-generated `RESULTS.md` says "Anomalies: none".**
  - That check covers only the uniqueness of the demand md5. It misses P1 and P3.
  - It also does not report the total effect in %, the Shapley shares, seed counts, coordination minus network, or
    any comparison with round 5. This report adds them.
  - Its numbers are correct. For example: total effect 1072.92 ± 39.67 and 1412.24 ± 148.42 USD; network value under
    the best rule 835.84 ± 84.57 USD.
- **P3. Five network r2 rows violate the report-only family (9).**
  - The rows are usd4h seed 9 and stress4h seeds 0, 1, 2 and 4: `check_ok = False`, `ok_modulo_report_only = True`.
    These are the same five rows as in round 5 and are kept by protocol.
  - Under the new prices, only one best-rule cell uses such a schedule: stress4h seed 0, network (r2 1184.0 vs M2+M3
    1307.4 USD). In round 5 there were two such cells. On usd4h seed 9, M2+M3 (879.2) now beats r2 (880.3).
- **P4. M2 and M2+M3 are identical on all 30 instance-infrastructure pairs.** No instance has services, so the M3
  layer is inactive. In effect, the best rule is the minimum of r2 and M2. Where the tables show "M2+M3", M2 has the
  same schedule.
- **P5. The rule schedules are identical to round 5. Only their prices changed.**
  - Served orders, bike km, swaps, empty km and response cost are equal on all 90 rule rows.
  - The repricing identities hold on 90/90 rows: lateness(new) = lateness(old) x 15/36, and unserved = 60 x
    n_unserved.
  - Every change in a rule-based quantity between rounds is therefore repricing plus re-selection of the best rule.
  - The best rule changed on 5 of the 10 usd4h seeds (at home on seeds 2 and 5; on the network on seeds 0, 2, 4 and
    9) and on no stress4h seed.
- **P6. These instances are shared with other stages, so they are not new evidence.**
  - The usd4h network cell is the R6 headline usd4h x1 instance set. Its rule rows are identical, and its ILP rows
    agree within 0.06 USD (alternative optima with different end adjustments). The usd4h gap reported here is the
    headline gap again.
  - The stress4h network cell is the psweep P 60 stress4h cell on seeds 0-4.
- **P7. At v 15 the ILP chooses more late deliveries.**
  - Late orders per window, ILP, new vs old prices: network 3.1 vs 0.6 on usd4h and 10.8 vs 0.4 on stress4h; home 7.3
    vs 0.4 and 11.2 vs 0.2.
  - No ILP order is more than 60 min late. At most 1.0 order per window is more than 30 min late.
  - This is the intended effect of cheaper lateness, but part of the ILP's gain in served share is late service.
- **P8. USD effects are not comparable across the two price sets.** The objective itself changed. Per window, the
  home best-rule cell rose by 619 USD (usd4h) and 707 USD (stress4h), and the home ILP cell by 309 and 630 USD,
  mostly through the doubled penalty on unserved orders. Compare the two rounds through the %
  rows and the operating metrics. Even the % rows depend on prices.
- **P9. Caveats carried over from round 5:**
  - The ILP outage rate (0.38 on usd4h, 0.63 on stress4h for the network ILP) is mostly the reserve-breach flag.
  - Home electricity is not priced (`C_elec = 0` on every home row).
  - The network ILP with written targets is not part of this stage (phase a only), so the price of recovery was not
    re-measured under the new prices.
  - The 15 home written-target rows are implied by construction and were not solved. They are not used here.
- **P10. Round-5 records lack the 30-min and 60-min lateness counts,** so those two counts have no old-price
  comparison.
- **P11. The round-5 report's headline tables used the raw objective (26).** Here both rounds use the end-adjusted
  basis.
  - Recomputed on the end-adjusted basis, round-5 numbers move by at most 1.5 USD.
  - The round-5 raw-objective numbers reproduce exactly from its records, for example: total effect 474.3
    [390.3, 558.3] USD; network value under the ILP 17.7 % [16.2, 19.2]; usd4h seed 8 network value under the best
    rule -179.9 USD.

## 3. Answer in brief

1. **The total effect grows.** Going from (home charging, best rule) to (swap network, ILP) saves:
   - usd4h: 1072.9 USD [1033.3, 1112.6] per 4-h window = 64.1 % [62.5, 65.6] of the home best-rule cost (n = 10,
     10/10 seeds).
   - stress4h: 1412.2 USD [1263.8, 1560.7] = 60.0 % [54.5, 65.4] (n = 5, 5/5 seeds).
   - Old prices: 44.3 % [39.5, 49.0] and 49.5 % [42.6, 56.5]. Paired change: +19.8 [15.9, 23.7] and +10.4
     [6.3, 14.6] percentage points.
2. **The Shapley split reverses.** Under the new prices the network is the larger lever.
   - usd4h: network 624.8 USD [579.6, 670.1] (58.2 % [55.1, 61.3] of the total) vs coordination 448.1 [413.4, 482.7].
     Coordination minus network is -176.8 USD [-247.0, -106.5], and coordination is larger on 0/10 seeds.
   - Round 5 on usd4h: coordination was larger, +124.4 [32.9, 215.8] USD, on 8/10 seeds.
   - stress4h: network 761.8 [601.2, 922.3] (53.8 % [46.4, 61.1]) vs coordination 650.5 [546.6, 754.3]. The
     difference, -111.3 [-337.4, 114.7] (2/5 seeds), is not significant. Round 5: +324.7 [134.1, 515.2], 5/5 seeds.
   - Paired change in the network's Shapley share: +23.2 [10.6, 35.7] points (usd4h) and +24.6 [16.0, 33.2] points
     (stress4h).
3. **The network's value under the ILP more than doubles in relative terms.**
   - usd4h: 413.9 USD [367.3, 460.4] = 40.6 % [37.7, 43.4] of the home ILP cost (10/10 seeds). Old prices: 17.6 %
     [16.1, 19.1]. Paired change +23.0 [21.4, 24.6] points.
   - stress4h: 751.1 [691.3, 810.9] = 44.5 % [39.5, 49.4] (5/5). Old prices: 22.3 %. Paired change +22.2
     [19.9, 24.5] points.
   - The whole of it comes from avoided penalties on unserved orders (Table 5).
4. **The network's value under the best rule is no longer negative on any seed.**
   - usd4h: 835.8 USD [751.3, 920.4] = 49.9 % [45.0, 54.9] of the home cost, positive on 10/10 seeds (minimum
     646.2 USD, seed 8).
   - stress4h: 772.5 [481.1, 1063.9] = 32.8 % [20.4, 45.2], positive on 5/5 (minimum 576.2, seed 1).
   - In round 5, usd4h seed 8 was -180.0 USD. With identical rule schedules it is now +646.2 USD. The r2-only network
     value on stress4h seed 1 (-66.2 USD in round 5) is now +300.3 USD. Round-5 artefact (d) was caused by the prices.
5. **The coordination value on the network (the gap) shows no detectable change in its mean.** This does not show that
   it is unchanged (corrected in verification, see the last section).
   - usd4h (n = 10, all 10 network ILPs OPTIMAL in both rounds): 27.3 % [21.7, 33.0] at new prices vs 28.3 %
     [20.9, 35.6] at old prices. Paired -0.9 [-5.8, 3.9] points, higher on 5/10 seeds. Individual seeds move by
     -10.9 (seed 8) to +9.9 (seed 4) points.
   - stress4h (n = 5): 40.0 % [34.6, 45.4] vs 40.0 % [33.6, 46.3]. Paired 0.0 [-3.7, 3.7] points, higher on 2/5
     seeds; per-seed changes -3.4 to +3.1 points.
   - Solve status on stress4h: at new prices only seeds 0 and 4 are OPTIMAL; seeds 1, 2 and 3 stopped at the 300 s
     TIME_LIMIT (MIP gaps 1.12 %, 0.31 %, 0.43 %). All 5 old-price ILPs were OPTIMAL. The new-price stress4h gap is
     therefore slightly understated: on the MIP bound it is at most 40.2 % [34.7, 45.7], paired +0.3 [-3.2, 3.7] (P1).
   - With n = 10 and n = 5 the paired intervals cannot exclude shifts of about 4-6 points. Only the percentage is flat:
     in USD the stress4h coordination value on the network rose by 78.5 [-15.0, 171.9] USD, on 5/5 seeds (Table 4).
   - What moved is coordination at home: +7.0 [2.7, 11.3] points on usd4h, and -7.1 [-14.1, -0.1] points on stress4h
     (only 1/5 seeds higher, p = 0.048).
6. **The two levers are substitutes.**
   - usd4h: the interaction (coordination value on the network - at home) is -422.0 USD [-524.1, -319.8], negative on
     10/10 seeds. In round 5 it was -100.4 [-243.1, 42.3], not significant.
   - stress4h: -21.4 [-293.1, 250.3], indistinguishable from zero, as in round 5.
7. **Productivity: the network buys rider time, not distance. This holds under both price sets.**
   - ILP on the network vs ILP at home, orders served per rider-hour: 1.48 vs 1.11 on usd4h (paired +0.37
     [0.33, 0.42]) and 1.91 vs 1.18 on stress4h (+0.73 [0.65, 0.80]).
   - Empty km per rider-hour rises: +0.53 [0.35, 0.71] on usd4h (0.76 vs 0.23) and +0.64 [0.44, 0.85] on stress4h
     (0.82 vs 0.18).
   - Under the new prices, both ILP cells serve more orders than in round 5: network served share 0.989 vs 0.953 and
     0.954 vs 0.875.

## 5. Comparison with round 5 (old prices, same 15 instances)

- **Unchanged:**
  - The demand, the instances and the rule schedules (P5).
  - The sign and rough size of the productivity contrasts. The network roughly doubles orders per rider-hour under
    the rules (+0.81, +0.74) and adds +0.37 to +0.73 under the ILP. Empty km per rider-hour goes up, not down, on the
    network.
- **No detectable change (not shown to be unchanged):**
  - The coordination value on the network: 27.3 % vs 28.3 % (usd4h, n = 10, all ILPs OPTIMAL, paired -0.9
    [-5.8, 3.9], 5/10 seeds higher) and 40.0 % vs 40.0 % (stress4h, n = 5, paired 0.0 [-3.7, 3.7], 2/5 seeds higher).
    Mean differences are under 1 point, but the CIs allow shifts of up to about 6 points and single seeds move by up
    to 11 points (usd4h). On stress4h, 3 of the 5 new-price network ILPs are TIME_LIMIT incumbents and all 5
    old-price ILPs are OPTIMAL (P1); on the MIP bound the paired difference is +0.3 [-3.2, 3.7].
- **Changed by the new prices:**
  - The home cells become much more expensive, because the home rule drops 22-30 orders per window, each now
    costing 60 USD instead of 30.
  - As a result, the network value grows in both USD and %: under the ILP 17.6 → 40.6 % and 22.3 → 44.5 %; under the
    best rule 20.3 → 49.9 % and 15.2 → 32.8 %.
  - The total effect grows: 44.3 → 64.1 % and 49.5 → 60.0 %.
  - The Shapley ranking reverses: the network share goes from 35 % to 58 % on usd4h and from 29 % to 54 % on stress4h.
  - The usd4h interaction becomes clearly negative (-422 USD, 0/10 seeds positive).
- **Round-5 artefact (d) is resolved:**
  - The negative rule-based network value on usd4h seed 8 came entirely from the old prices.
  - Under the old prices, the network rule's 22 late orders cost 312 USD and its 2 unserved orders 60 USD. The home
    rule's 24 unserved orders cost 720 USD. Home: 936.8 USD; network: 1116.8 USD.
  - Under the new prices, the same schedules cost 1641.0 USD (home: 24 × 60 = 1440 unserved) and 994.8 USD (network:
    lateness 312 × 15/36 = 130). The network value is +646.2 USD.
  - No seed of either family has a negative network value under any rule (minimum 300.3 USD, r2 on stress4h seed 1).
- **The ILP's behaviour shifted:**
  - It now serves more orders: served share +0.036 (network) and +0.083 (home) on usd4h; +0.079 and +0.092 on
    stress4h.
  - It accepts more late deliveries in exchange (P7).
  - The ILP's lead over the rule on the network shows no detectable change in % (see above). The new prices move the
    network best-rule and network ILP costs by similar relative amounts: +1.8 % [-4.3, 7.9] vs +3.3 % [1.1, 5.4] on

    proportional rise is a coincidence of the two cost mixes, not a consequence of near-full service.

## 6. Interpretation

What these records show (same model and instances; 6 bikes, 3 co-located swap-and-charge sites, no services):

1. Both levers save a lot, and both are positive on every seed under the new prices. Together they cut the window
   cost by about 60-64 % relative to home charging run by the best rule.
2. Under the new prices the network is the larger lever on usd4h (Shapley 625 vs 448 USD; coordination - network
   -177 [-247, -107], 0/10 seeds). On stress4h the point estimates point the same way, but the difference is not
   significant at n = 5. This reverses the round-5 statement that "coordination is the larger lever (63 % / 70 %)".
   That statement was conditional on P 30 and should not be carried into the paper.
3. The network's value is, in cost terms, avoided penalties on unserved orders. Home charging (a 125-min recharge
   per pack) caps service at 38 % under the rules and 59-74 % under the ILP. The network lifts it to 75-99 %. The
   USD value therefore scales with P. With operations fixed, doubling P doubles the unserved-penalty component, from
   603 to 1170 USD under the best rule on usd4h.
4. The network's value under the rule is no longer negative on any seed. The round-5 negative seed was a pricing
   artefact, demonstrated directly: identical schedules, opposite sign.
5. Coordination's relative value on the network (the paper's gap: 27 % on usd4h, n = 10; 40 % on stress4h, n = 5)
   shows no detectable change between the two price sets: paired -0.9 [-5.8, 3.9] and 0.0 [-3.7, 3.7] points. It is
   the only percentage in Table 3 whose paired CI contains 0 on both families. This does not show price invariance:
   the CIs allow shifts of several points, single usd4h seeds move by up to 11 points, only two price points were
   compared, and 3 of the 5 new-price stress4h network ILPs are TIME_LIMIT incumbents (P1).
6. The two levers are substitutes. On usd4h the ILP recovers at home much of what the network provides
   (coordination value at home 659 USD vs 237 USD on the network).
7. The network buys rider time, not distance. Under the ILP, orders per rider-hour rise by 0.37-0.73 and empty km per
   rider-hour by 0.53-0.64.

What is suggested but not shown here:

- *Which lever is larger depends on the penalty for unserved orders.* On usd4h the crossover lies somewhere between
  P 30 and P 60. This stage cannot locate it: only two price points were run in the 2 x 2, and psweep covers the
  network infrastructure only.
- *How sensitive the network's value is to the home-charging counterfactual* is not tested. The counterfactual uses a
  125-min recharge, 0.24 kW, a return at 25 % and unpriced home electricity. A faster home charger, or second packs
  at home, would shrink the network's value.
- *Whether the larger ILP service gain under the new prices is desirable* depends on whether late service (up to 30-60
  min) is acceptable. The objective now says it is. A service-level view would count 3.1 (usd4h) or 10.8 (stress4h)
  late orders per window on the network ILP, against 0.6 and 0.4 under the old prices.
- *Generalisation* to larger fleets, transport services or other demand levels is not covered: 6 bikes, no services,
  n = 5 on stress4h. The usd4h cell repeats the headline instances (P6) and is not independent evidence.

Decision: the refuters are right. Every number in the claim reproduces exactly, so no number, table or figure changes.
The fault was in the wording and in what the claim left out:
1. "Does not change" or "does not move" was stated as equality. The data show only that no change in the mean can be
   detected. With n = 10 and n = 5 the paired CIs allow shifts of about -5.8 to +3.9 points (usd4h) and ±3.7 points

   depend on the price change" go beyond that evidence: only two price points were compared.
2. The claim gave neither n nor solve status. At new prices, 3 of the 5 stress4h network ILPs are TIME_LIMIT
   incumbents, against 5/5 OPTIMAL at old prices. So the new-price stress4h gap is slightly understated, and the
   paired comparison sets time-limited solves against optimal ones. On the bound the conclusion holds (paired +0.25).
3. Only the percentage is flat. On stress4h the USD coordination value rose on 5/5 seeds; the percentage stays flat
   because the network best-rule cost rose by a similar proportion.

One detail in refuter 1's evidence is wrong: it says the best rule differs between price sets on 3 of the 10 usd4h
seeds. On the network it differs on 4 seeds (0, 2, 4 and 9), as P5 states (at home on seeds 2 and 5).
