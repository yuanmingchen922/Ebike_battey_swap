## Summary

The perfect-information ILP is far cheaper than every online policy: the conservative gap of `best_rule` to the relaxed ILP is 28.97 +- 7.27 % on `nyc4h_stress2` and 41.30 +- 2.61 % on `r5_stress4h` (end-adjusted, n = 10 each). The PPO station agent does not close this gap. On `nyc4h_stress2` its cost is statistically indistinguishable from its own rule station layer (R2 riders +22.07 +- 25.89 USD, R5 riders -8.38 +- 18.87 USD), but it serves fewer orders than that layer (-3.6 +- 3.1 points with R2 riders, -5.6 +- 3.6 with R5 riders) and buys 1.0-1.5 kWh less energy; it is not cheaper than `best_rule` (point estimates +61.01 +- 65.38 and +46.34 +- 47.92 USD, CIs include 0, so not significantly worse either). On `r5_stress4h` the R5-rider agent is 149.69 +- 58.13 USD per window cheaper than `best_rule` (10 of 10 seeds), but it achieves this by collapsing to the "never charge" heuristic: its per-seed end-adjusted cost equals `heuristic_none[r5]` to within 0.33 USD (mean difference +0.03 +- 0.07), and the R2-rider agent equals `heuristic_none[r2]` to within 2e-13 USD on every seed. Its served share falls to 58.5 % (best_rule 74.6 %, ILP 85.4 %). The saving is therefore a property of the objective and the rider layer on the stressed family (a dropped order costs less than a very late one), not learned station control. Price of recovery (written - relaxed ILP, end-adjusted) is 0.33 +- 0.54 USD on `nyc4h_stress2` (all optimal) and 4.74 +- 4.98 USD on `r5_stress4h` (time-limited incumbents).

## Anomalies

1. Policy collapse: on `r5_stress4h` the deterministic PPO policy is "never charge" in outcome for both rider layers (R2: identical end-adjusted cost on 10/10 seeds; R5: max difference 0.33 USD; kWh bought 0.12 / 0.24 vs 0). The "RL beats best_rule by 149.69 USD" result is therefore the never-charge heuristic, not learned control.
2. Objective artefact: never charging beats every rule on `r5_stress4h` with R5 riders (1291.36 vs best_rule 1441.08 USD) while serving 16 points fewer orders (58.5 % vs 74.6 %). The rules accept orders they deliver very late (lateness 303.9 USD for best_rule vs 137.7 for never-charge, 2.1 for the ILP); dropping an order costs 30 USD, which is cheaper than its response + lateness cost under the rules. This needs a sentence in the paper (or a service-level constraint) before RL numbers are reported.
3. The raw first-100 / last-100 training means are confounded by instance mix (corrected in the digest). The training instance of every episode was reconstructed from the environment's seeded draw (gymnasium `np_random(0)`, 16 consecutive episodes per draw from seeds 1000-1019; instance explains R^2 = 0.74-0.99 of episode cost vs 0.04-0.07 for a random assignment). The instance mix alone moves the last-100 block by +20.0 / -110.8 / +200.5 / +53.6 USD (nyc R2 / nyc R5 / r5 R2 / r5 R5). With instance fixed effects, the last-100 minus first-100 change is -82.8 [-99.1, -66.5] (nyc4h_stress2 R2), -175.0 [-198.9, -151.0] (nyc4h_stress2 R5), -46.6 [-50.7, -42.4] (r5_stress4h R2, about -3 %) and -53.1 [-57.8, -48.4] USD (r5_stress4h R5, about -4 %) (OLS intervals treat episodes as independent and are too narrow). So the stochastic training policy improved in all four runs, clearly on nyc4h_stress2 and slightly on r5_stress4h; the apparent worsening for R2 riders on r5_stress4h (1428.0 -> 1584.7 raw) is harder instances in the last block. On r5_stress4h the deterministic test policy nevertheless behaves as never-charge. One PPO seed, no hyper-parameter tuning, 200 k steps.
4. Training runs at 124-129 steps/s and 6.7-6.9 GB peak RSS on `r5_stress4h` (3.1 M arcs per instance) vs 410-432 steps/s and 2.1 GB on `nyc4h_stress2`.
5. Price of recovery is negative on 2 `r5_stress4h` seeds (seed 5: -0.19, seed 9: -2.20 USD) and up to 21.51 USD (seed 4, written ILP TIME_LIMIT 2.52 %): both ILPs are certified optimal on only 1 of 10 seeds there, so this number is incumbent noise. On `nyc4h_stress2` it is 0.33 +- 0.54 USD (tiny negatives -0.02 to -0.05 USD arise because the ILP optimises (26), not the end-adjusted cost).
6. `nyc4h_stress2` ILP uses PRUNING_NYC (binds only the ILP), so its optimum is an upper bound on the written optimum and the reported gaps are conservative.
7. Context: `best_rule` gap on `nyc4h_stress2` (28.97 +- 7.27 %) is in the range of the round-3/4 `nyc4h` figure (31.8 +- 8.2 %, n = 10) but is a different (stressed) family; no direct comparison is implied.

## Interpretation

The ILP remains the reference that matters: with perfect information it saves 29 % (nyc4h_stress2) and 41 % (r5_stress4h) of the cost of the best per-seed rule, largely by eliminating lateness (6.0 and 2.1 USD vs 181.2 and 303.9 USD) at an equal or higher served share. A PPO agent that controls only the station layer cannot recover this, because the large cost terms (order assignment, rider timing) are set by the rule rider layer it sits on. On the semi-real `nyc4h_stress2` family the agent learns a charging policy whose cost matches its own rule within the noise (-8.38 +- 18.87 USD for R5 riders, +22.07 +- 25.89 USD for R2 riders) while serving 3.6-5.6 points fewer orders and buying 1.0-1.5 kWh less; it beats never charging by about 108 USD (significant for R2 riders, -108.44 +- 77.55, p = 0.01; not for R5 riders, -109.31 +- 128.82, p = 0.09), and it does not beat the per-seed best rule (point estimates 46-61 USD dearer, CIs include 0). On the stressed synthetic family the agent converges to never charging, which is cheaper than any rule in E2 terms only because unserved orders are priced below late ones there. That apparent 149.69 +- 58.13 USD gain comes with a 16-point loss in served share and should be reported as an objective-calibration effect, not as an RL win. Generalisation from training seeds 1000-1019 to test seeds 0-9 is not a binding issue: the r5_stress4h policy is effectively instance-independent, and on nyc4h_stress2 the deterministic test cost (971.8 R5 / 986.5 R2) lies within the spread of the training curve. Once the training instance mix is controlled for, training lowered episode cost in all four runs (nyc4h_stress2 -83 and -175 USD; r5_stress4h -47 and -53 USD, 3-4 %), but on r5_stress4h the deterministic policy still behaves as never-charge, so 200 k steps with one PPO seed and default hyper-parameters is the limit of what this comparator shows. The paired CIs (n = 10) are wide on nyc4h_stress2 (about +-20 to +-65 USD), so only large effects can be resolved. For the manuscript the defensible statement is: a learned station policy on top of rule riders does not approach the coordinated ILP (gap to the relaxed ILP 31.77-42.85 % for PPO vs 28.97-41.30 % for best_rule), and where it "beats" rules it does so by refusing service. A service-level constraint or a higher unserved penalty would be needed before an RL comparison on r5_stress4h is meaningful.
